Overview

A closed table for the people who run the region's networks

The APAN/Asi@Connect Leaders Forum (AALF), inaugurated at APAN56 in Colombo, brings together C-level executives from National Research and Education Networks (NRENs) across Asia-Pacific. It is a closed, high-level event built to strengthen collaboration, work through shared challenges, and shape a common vision for sustainable digital infrastructure in support of research and education.

The forum was originally formed as the NREN Leaders Forum at APAN56. With its continued growth and broader regional collaboration, it has been known as the APAN/Asi@Connect Leaders Forum (AALF) since APAN61.

About the forum

Convened by Prof. Roshan Ragel, CEO of LEARN, the forum offers a recurring platform for REN leaders to engage in high-level dialogue during APAN meetings. It supports strategic vision, policy development, technology leadership, and cross-border cooperation.

Key objectives

What the forum is working toward

  • Facilitate discussion on the challenges and opportunities RENs face.
  • Share insight on emerging technologies and trends relevant to the REN community.
  • Foster collaboration and partnerships among RENs.
  • Explore strategies for strengthening the sustainability and impact of RENs.
  • Develop a collective vision for the future of RENs across Asia-Pacific.
Target participants

Who sits at the table

REN executives

C-level executives from National Research and Education Networks across Asia-Pacific.

Global REN leaders

Leaders and decision-makers from TEIN*CC, NORDUnet, GÉANT, APNIC, and Internet2, as invitees.

Institutional stakeholders

Governmental and non-governmental organisations engaged in research and education networking.

Technology partners

Representatives from technology providers relevant to REN infrastructure.
